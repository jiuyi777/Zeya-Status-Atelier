# P1 旅行手账封面

生成方式：内置 image_gen，2026-10-07。
用途：拼贴手账的完整封面，3:2 固定比例；可编辑标题使用独立 HTML 叠纸，正文纸面和票根由 CSS 随内容延长。
实装：travel-cover-v2.webp，1200 × 800，WebP 压缩；图片独立引用，不嵌入角色卡。

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original richly composed travel scrapbook collage artwork, landscape 3:2, for the substantial cover of a Chinese story journal interface. Full-bleed flat-lay tactile paper assemblage, NOT a website mockup. A large faded analog photograph of an old European seaside town with terracotta roofs and harbor occupies the central 60%, tilted slightly, cream photo border. Overlapping under and around it: old ochre street map, warm cream torn notebook paper, faded burgundy railway ticket, two large perforated illustrated postage stamps, translucent brown washi tape, a little dark red wax stamp impression and a botanical pressed sprig. The objects overlap across the ENTIRE composition, from left to right, with deliberate visual hierarchy and rich paper shadows, not a lone sticker at a corner. Warm cream, weathered tobacco brown, dusty navy, restrained oxblood red. Photoreal paper textures but refined art-directed editorial collage, gentle diffuse daylight, aged but clean, not dirty or grungy. Crop several background paper layers at edges to feel abundant. Main harbor photo should be vivid enough to read at 350px width. No human figures. No UI, no buttons, no large text, no legible words, no gibberish lettering. Tiny nonverbal ticket rules and postmark rings allowed. Bottom edge a continuous warm cream paper layer to allow a separate HTML title sheet to overlap the artwork in the final interface. No blue gradient, no watercolor floral banner, no flat vector drawing.
