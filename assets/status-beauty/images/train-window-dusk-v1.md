Prompt:
Create a refined painterly cinematic illustration for a mobile character status panel called twilight train. Wide landscape 3:2 image, view from inside a vintage passenger train through a single large rounded rectangular window, fine aged brushed brass trim and dark muted blue green carriage wall framing all four sides, thin central vertical mullion. Outside: atmospheric twilight mountains, river reflecting amber sunset, distant scattered village lights, nuanced cloudy peach-grey sky. Detailed material texture and subtle glass reflections, elegant and realistic yet softly illustrated, restrained dusty teal and warm sand palette, not flat vector shapes. No people, no writing, no letters, no numbers, no logos. Window fills most of image, little surrounding wall. Image will be displayed at 350px width above editable status text; do not put any UI or text in the image.
